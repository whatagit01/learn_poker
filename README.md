# Pot Logic ♠️

All amounts are in **big blinds (bb)** and bet sizes are shown as a **% of the pot**.

A small web app that does the poker maths for you and then tells you the **next best action** and why.

## Features

**Analyse:** a visual poker table at the top. Tap to add your hole cards and the board; it names your hand, counts your draw outs, shows your win % against 1–5 random hands, and seats the opponents (who bet, who called, who's still to act) for **multiway** spots. **Deal:** pick any cards you want (hole cards, flop, turn, river) and the opponent count, or none at all; Deal keeps your choices and randomises everything else. Then you decide to fold, call or raise on the flop, turn and river *before* the maths is shown, then see whether you matched it, your real equity against the opponents' actual cards, and the showdown. Or enter the pot, villain's bet, your stack and either your outs or your equity. You get:
- **Verdict:** Call / Fold / Raise / Shove / Call on implied odds / Semi-bluff shove
- Pot odds and required equity, shown against your equity on a bar
- Call EV in chips
- **SPR** now and after calling, with stack-off guidance
- Implied odds needed (how much more you must win later)
- Fold equity a semi-bluff shove needs
- MDF and bluff break-even for the current bet size
- A step-by-step explanation of each number

**Practice:** endless **generated** questions. No fixed question bank. Each question builds a random spot, works out the answer with the same maths engine, and makes the wrong answers from common mistakes. Topics: pot odds, counting outs, outs to equity, SPR, MDF, bluffing, best action, hand vs hand, multiway pot odds, multiway equity, playing draws and stakes adjustments. Questions show the cards and seats on a mini table. Questions adapt to a **low / medium stakes** setting.

**Learn:** short explanations and formulas for every concept, plus exact win percentages for common all-in matchups, preflop and on the flop (for example QQ vs AK, set vs overpair, top pair vs combo draw).

## The maths

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Required equity | call ÷ (pot + bet + call) |
| Equity, 1 card | outs ÷ unseen cards (47 flop, 46 turn) |
| Equity, flop → river | 1 − C(47−outs, 2) ÷ C(47, 2) |
| SPR | effective stack ÷ pot |
| Implied odds needed | call ÷ equity − (pot + bet + call) |
| MDF | pot ÷ (pot + bet) |
| Bluff break-even | bet ÷ (pot + bet) |
| Shove fold equity needed | −EV(called) ÷ (pot + bet − EV(called)) |

On the flop, the app uses two-card equity only when the call puts you all-in. Otherwise you still have to pay on the turn, so it uses one card.

## Files

- `poker-math.js`: pure maths and the decision engine
- `poker-eval.js`: hand evaluator, hand naming, draw-out detection, exact and Monte Carlo equity
- `quiz.js`: question generators
- `matchups.js`: exact matchup equities, generated by `node tools/compute-matchups.js` (full enumeration of every runout)
- `app.js`: UI
- `index.html`, `style.css`
- `test.js`: tests
